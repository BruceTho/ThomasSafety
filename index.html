Safety is a practice

Thomas Safety Institute, LLC helps drivers turn sound safety principles into habits they can use on every trip—from the driveway to the busiest intersection.

Four habits that matter

Coach the choices.
Change the drive.

Practical guidance helps drivers see what’s developing around them and choose a safer next move.

01

Create space

Keep a safe following distance, allow room beside your vehicle, and leave yourself options when conditions change.

02

Limit reversing

Plan ahead, choose a forward exit when possible, and use a deliberate, observant routine whenever backing is needed.

03

Approach intersections

Ease off early, scan in every direction, and be ready for people or vehicles that may enter your path.

04

Limit distractions

Set up before moving, keep attention on the road, and make focused driving the priority for every trip.

Awareness into action
